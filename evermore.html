Evermore – Mobile Game Art, 3D Assets, Tech Art & UI Design


We’re excited to showcase our collaboration with Evermore on their mobile game, where our team contributed across several key areas to help bring the project to life.


Concept Art
Our concept artists crafted and iterated on the visual foundation of the game—designing characters, environments, and props to establish a distinctive and engaging art style.


3D Assets
Our 3D team translated these concepts into detailed, game-ready models optimized for mobile performance, ensuring both beauty and efficiency.


Prop Design & Tech Art
We designed a variety of in-game props and supported the pipeline with tech art solutions—streamlining asset integration, optimizing performance, and enhancing visual fidelity.


Game UI
Our UI designers created intuitive and visually cohesive interface elements that align with the game’s aesthetic and enhance the overall user experience.
