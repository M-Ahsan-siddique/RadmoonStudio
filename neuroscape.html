CARD ILLUSTRATIONS

Our team had the incredible opportunity to work on Neuroscape, a visually captivating project that blends art and storytelling through a richly imagined card game. We created dozens of custom illustrations, each designed to bring the game’s unique world and characters to life. The project resonated with a global audience, ultimately raising over $500,000 USD on Kickstarter. We had an amazing time collaborating and bringing these illustrations to life, and we're proud to have contributed to such a successful and creative venture.
